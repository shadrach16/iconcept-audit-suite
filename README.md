## 📖 Project Overview

**iConcept4Pro** is an enterprise-grade Internal Control and Audit workflow software designed specifically for the rigorous demands of the Nigerian Banking Sector. 

It serves as a central nervous system for Internal Auditors and Control Officers, replacing fragmented manual processes with a unified, automated, and secure digital environment. The system automates fraud detection, regulatory reporting, and exception management across hundreds of bank branches.

---

## ❓ The Problem

Before iConcept4Pro, major financial institutions faced critical operational risks:
* **Fragmented Data:** Internal controls relied on disjointed spreadsheets and manual emails.
* **Delayed Detection:** Fraud and exceptions were often caught weeks after occurrence due to manual "Call Over" processes.
* **Regulatory Gaps:** complying with rapidly changing NFIU and CBN policies was manual and error-prone.
* **Audit Trails:** Lack of a unified, immutable digital audit trail for auditor continuity.

---

## 💡 The Solution

We built a full-stack, multi-tenant solution that handles **Continuous Transaction Monitoring** and **Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)**.

### Core Modules
* **🕵️‍♂️ Fraud & Exception Management:** Real-time monitoring engine that flags suspicious transactions based on behavioral analysis.
* **📜 Audit Library & Continuity:** A digital repository ensuring audit procedures are standardized and preserved across personnel changes.
* **🤖 Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM) & CAM:** Integrated engines for Certified Anti-Money Laundering checks.
* **⚡ Automated Call Over:** Replaces manual ticket checking with automated verification logic.
* **📢 Intelligent Escalation:** Automated email messaging and alert routing based on severity levels.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

The system was architected to handle high-volume banking transactions with zero downtime.

* **Frontend:** `React.js` (Dynamic dashboards, complex forms, data visualization)
* **Backend:** `Python Django` (Robust ORM, secure authentication, heavy business logic)
* **Database:** Multi-Database Support including `Oracle DB` and `MSSQL` (Critical for banking legacy integration).
* **Security:** JWT Authentication, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Independent IT Audit Access channels.

### System Highlights
- **Multi-DB Architecture:** Seamlessly connects to various core banking databases (Oracle, MSSQL) for parallel data processing.
- **Custom Parameterization:** Allows banks to define their own risk thresholds and rule sets without code changes.
